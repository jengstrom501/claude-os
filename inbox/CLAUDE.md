# Inbox

Unsorted dropzone. Drop anything here — links, notes, files, half-formed ideas — without
deciding where it belongs yet.

Say **"sort my inbox"** and the agent will, for each item, propose a destination
(`explore/<topic>/`, `build/<topic>/`, or `manage/<area>/`), confirm with you, move it.

Nothing durable should stay here — the inbox is a staging area, not storage.
