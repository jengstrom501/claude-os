# Explore

Research and learning: topics explored to understand them, not to ship them. One directory per
topic, isolated like any project.

## Working here
- Topic = `CLAUDE.md` (scope, sources to trust) + `STATUS.md` (questions, findings, open threads).
- Research loop: split into independent questions → parallel `worker`s with sources → orchestrator
  synthesizes. Every finding carries a source; conclusions stay in this topic.
- Long notes go in `notes/`; STATUS.md links to them and holds the current conclusions.
